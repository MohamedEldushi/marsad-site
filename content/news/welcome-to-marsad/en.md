Marsad is a small, independent game studio. We build small, focused worlds, and we take our time with each one until it's worth the time it asks of the people who play it.

## Small worlds, on purpose

Every game here starts from a single idea: a mechanic, a mood, or a question. We don't add what it doesn't need, and we don't release it before that one idea is done right.

## Arabic first

Most games made for Arabic-speaking players are built in English and translated later. We work the other way around: we design in Arabic first, then write the English version as a genuine second language. That changes real decisions:

- How much space every heading and every button needs.
- Which way the interface runs, and what mirrors and what doesn't.
- The rhythm of a sentence in a menu or a line of dialogue.

> We want Arabic-speaking players to feel a game was written for them, not translated for them.
