Thank you to everyone playing the Salt & Signal beta. This build is mostly about the feel of the sea, and about making the signals easier to follow.

## What's new

- Sailing into the wind is smoother, and turning no longer stalls the boat.
- Signals now pulse more clearly when you're heading the right way.
- The logbook keeps every signal you've found, with the date and your position.

## Fixed

- The compass no longer points the wrong way after loading a save.
- Night and fog no longer hide the logbook text.

## Tell us what you find

Found something odd at sea? [Send us a message](/support) and choose Salt & Signal. The more detail, the faster we can fix it.
